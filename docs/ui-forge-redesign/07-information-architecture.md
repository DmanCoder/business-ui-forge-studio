# Recommended information architecture

## IA principles

- Organise around buyer decisions and project fit, not the internal list of technologies.
- Make work and proof easier to reach than long explanations of capability.
- Keep the top-level route set small enough to scan instantly.
- Add a page only when there is enough distinct, useful content to maintain it.
- Preserve useful insight content, but do not expose thin taxonomies as if they are mature destinations.
- Give every page one primary conversion role and a sensible next step.

## Primary navigation

1. Work
2. Services
3. Process
4. About
5. Insights
6. Start a project — visually distinguished CTA

The logo returns Home. Privacy and Terms stay in the footer. The contact CTA should use the same phrase across header, page closes, and mobile navigation unless testing proves a better label.

## Proposed route tree

```text

├── /work

├── /services
│   ├── /services/websites-and-commerce       (when content/proof is ready)
│   ├── /services/digital-products             (when content/proof is ready)
```

The three individual service routes should be staged, not assumed for launch. If each cannot contain unique proof, buying guidance, FAQs, and an appropriate case study, keep a single strong Services page.

## Page specifications

### Home — `/`

- **Purpose:** Establish position, demonstrate quality, create trust, and route visitors to relevant work or contact.
- **Primary audience:** First-time SME owners, founders, marketing leads, and product leads.
- **Main CTA:** Start a project.
- **Main sections:** Position/flagship evidence; proof strip; selected work; buyer outcomes; three service paths; founder advantage; process/artefacts; verified trust; fit/contact.
- **Conversion role:** Fastest path from unfamiliar visitor to “this studio appears relevant and credible”.

### Work — `/work`

- **Purpose:** Provide the strongest evidence of design and development capability.
- **Primary audience:** Visitors comparing providers and stakeholders seeking reassurance.
- **Main CTA:** View a relevant case study; page close CTA to start a project.
- **Main sections:** Work premise; featured projects; optional filters only after enough inventory; role/evidence legend; compact delivery principles; contact close.
- **Conversion role:** Move visitors from aesthetic interest to confidence in reasoning, execution, and outcomes.

### Case study — `/work/[project]`

- **Purpose:** Demonstrate how UI Forge thinks and delivers under real constraints.
- **Primary audience:** Qualified buyers, technical evaluators, and design-conscious decision-makers.
- **Main CTA:** Discuss a similar challenge.
- **Main sections:** Hero facts; client context; problem; constraints; approach; before/after; design system; responsive experience; technical implementation; results; quote; ownership/handover; related work; CTA.
- **Conversion role:** Replace claims with specific proof and reduce perceived delivery risk.

### Services hub — `/services`

- **Purpose:** Help buyers map their situation to an appropriate engagement.
- **Primary audience:** Visitors who know they need help but may not know a platform or project type.
- **Main CTA:** Discuss project fit.
- **Main sections:** Who/what the studio helps; three service paths; outcomes/deliverables; selection guidance; relevant work; approach; engagement boundaries; FAQs; CTA.
- **Conversion role:** Clarify fit and reduce confusion without presenting a sprawling menu.

### Websites & commerce — `/services/websites-and-commerce` (conditional)

- **Purpose:** Address marketing sites, content platforms, landing systems, and commerce builds as one outcome-oriented family.
- **Primary audience:** SMEs, marketing teams, and growing retailers.
- **Main CTA:** Discuss a website or store.
- **Main sections:** Situations; outcomes; platform decision guide; capabilities; relevant work; process; ownership/support; FAQs.
- **Conversion role:** Capture high-intent visitors while keeping WordPress, Webflow, HubSpot, Shopify, Hydrogen, and custom stacks subordinate to fit.

### Digital products — `/services/digital-products` (conditional)

- **Purpose:** Explain web applications, portals, dashboards, and mobile/product work.
- **Primary audience:** Founders and operational teams with a defined workflow or product problem.
- **Main CTA:** Explore product fit.
- **Main sections:** Product situations; discovery/scoping; UX/UI; architecture and build; integrations; QA/accessibility; relevant work; engagement model; FAQs.
- **Conversion role:** Demonstrate that UI Forge can handle complex interaction and implementation without overclaiming enterprise scale.

### Ongoing care — `/services/ongoing-care` (conditional)

- **Purpose:** Explain post-launch support, iteration, maintenance, and responsible ownership.
- **Primary audience:** Existing clients and teams with a neglected or evolving digital product.
- **Main CTA:** Discuss ongoing support.
- **Main sections:** Best-fit situations; what's included/excluded; response and prioritisation model; audit/transition path; reporting; ownership; FAQs.
- **Conversion role:** Create a credible recurring relationship and reduce fear of abandonment after launch.

### Process — `/process`

- **Purpose:** Make collaboration, decisions, responsibilities, and risk management concrete.
- **Primary audience:** Buyers evaluating how the engagement will feel and internal sponsors explaining it to colleagues.
- **Main CTA:** Bring a project into scoping.
- **Main sections:** Working principles; fit check; discovery; definition; design; build; QA; launch; handover/care; example artefacts; roles and responsibilities; common questions.
- **Conversion role:** Reduce uncertainty and show senior involvement without turning the process into generic agency theatre.

### About — `/about`

- **Purpose:** Establish accountable founder identity, experience, values, location, and operating model.
- **Primary audience:** Anyone evaluating personal fit and legitimacy.
- **Main CTA:** Start a conversation.
- **Main sections:** Founder introduction and portrait; why the studio exists; relevant experience; how founder-led delivery works; principles; network/partners if real; selected proof; location/availability.
- **Conversion role:** Make a one-person studio an advantage through visibility and specificity.

### Insights — `/insights`

- **Purpose:** Demonstrate useful judgment and answer pre-sale questions in plain language.
- **Primary audience:** Research-stage buyers and returning prospects.
- **Main CTA:** Read an article; contextual CTA to discuss the topic.
- **Main sections:** Editorial premise; featured article; current articles; topic navigation only once sufficiently populated; newsletter only if there is an actual publishing programme.
- **Conversion role:** Build search discovery and trust over a longer consideration cycle.

### Article — `/insights/[article]`

- **Purpose:** Give a definitive, buyer-friendly answer to one question.
- **Primary audience:** Search visitors and prospects resolving an objection.
- **Main CTA:** Topic-relevant project conversation or next article.
- **Main sections:** Clear answer; contents for long pieces; evidence/examples; definitions; practical checklist; related reading; contextual studio help; author identity/date/update policy.
- **Conversion role:** Convert expertise into trust without interruptive sales prompts.

### Start a project — `/start-a-project`

- **Purpose:** Capture enough information for a useful response while preserving momentum.
- **Primary audience:** Qualified or exploring prospects.
- **Main CTA:** Send enquiry.
- **Main sections:** Expectation/response note; essential fields; project type; problem/outcome; timing/budget as optional ranges if useful; privacy/consent; alternative contact; reassurance and next steps.
- **Conversion role:** Turn intent into a manageable, consented lead with less friction than the current long form.

### Thank you — `/thank-you`

- **Purpose:** Confirm receipt, set expectations, and give a useful next action.
- **Primary audience:** Submitted prospects.
- **Main CTA:** View a relevant case study or insight while waiting.
- **Main sections:** Confirmation; response timeframe; what happens next; urgent/alternative contact; selected useful links.
- **Conversion role:** Preserve confidence after submission and reduce duplicate messages.

### Privacy — `/privacy`

- **Purpose:** Provide accurate, approved privacy information for visitors and form submitters.
- **Primary audience:** All visitors, especially prospects submitting personal information.
- **Main CTA:** Contact for privacy questions, if appropriate.
- **Main sections:** Entity/contact; data collected; purpose/legal basis as applicable; providers; retention; cookies/analytics; rights; international processing; updates.
- **Conversion role:** Trust and compliance. The current placeholder page is a pre-launch blocker.

### Terms — `/terms`

- **Purpose:** State accurate website-use terms, distinguished from project contracts.
- **Primary audience:** Site visitors.
- **Main CTA:** None beyond a contact route for questions.
- **Main sections:** Entity; website use; intellectual property; acceptable use; information disclaimer; external links; liability; jurisdiction; updates.
- **Conversion role:** Trust and legal hygiene. The current placeholder page is a pre-launch blocker.

## Pages and structures not recommended now

- **Testimonials route:** Keep unpublished or redirect to Work until at least two verified, permissioned quotes exist. A generic 404 is not a useful public outcome.
- **Team page:** Not justified for a founder-led studio. Put the real person and operating model on About.
- **Top-level platform pages:** WordPress, Webflow, HubSpot, Shopify, and React are delivery options, not the primary buyer structure.
- **Generic Pricing page:** Do not publish without repeatable packages or defensible ranges. Explain scoping factors in Services and Insights instead.
- **Deep category taxonomy:** The current categories have too little content. Remove from the sitemap or set `noindex` until each represents a useful collection.
- **Separate Contact and Start pages:** One conversion route is enough.

## Conversion paths

### Path A — Referred visitor

Home → selected project → case study → Start a project.

### Path B — Search visitor with a buying question

Article → relevant service/work proof → Start a project.

### Path C — Service-comparison visitor

Services → service path or relevant case → Process → Start a project.

### Path D — Trust-sensitive SME owner

Home → About/founder → Process/ownership → Start a project.

### Path E — Technical evaluator

Work → case implementation chapter → Process/QA → Start a project.

Every path should expose a relevant next step without trapping the visitor in a modal, chat widget, or forced call booking.

## URL and migration notes

- Preserve current article and project URLs where possible; use permanent redirects for any change.
- Retain tested legacy redirects such as `/about-me`, `/contact`, `/blogs`, category legacy paths, and locale remnants while they still receive links/traffic.
- Avoid publishing conditional service routes until their content is complete; do not ship thin placeholders.
- Add canonical and sitemap coverage only for indexable, valuable pages.
- Review category indexing against actual content volume and search demand.
