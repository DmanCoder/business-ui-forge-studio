# Homepage redesign blueprint

## Homepage job

The homepage must establish UI Forge as a premium, founder-led design and development partner, prove the claim through real work, help the right buyer recognise their situation, and create a confident path to contact. It should be shorter and more varied than the current roughly 12,700px mobile page.

Target approximately eight major movements. The outline below separates sections for planning; adjacent sections may share one visual field in design.

## 1. Header and navigation

- **Goal:** Make orientation and contact effortless without competing with the hero.
- **Heading idea:** None.
- **Content required:** Wordmark; Work, Services, Process, About, Insights; Start a project CTA.
- **Visual treatment:** Paper surface, thin lower rule, compact height, strong focus states. A sticky state may become slightly denser after scroll.
- **Interaction/motion:** 120–180ms state feedback; no disappearing navigation. Mobile menu retains the current focus trap, Escape close, focus return, and scroll lock.
- **CTA:** Start a project.
- **Mobile:** Wordmark plus one obvious menu control with at least 44px target. Menu uses a full-height, readable hierarchy; contact and location can sit at the bottom without crowding primary links.

## 2. Hero — position plus flagship evidence

- **Goal:** Explain what UI Forge does, for whom, and why it is credible within one short scan.
- **Likely heading idea:** An outcome-led statement about designing and building important digital products with one accountable senior partner.
- **Content required:** One headline; one short supporting paragraph; primary and secondary CTA; one flagship project asset; a compact evidence line for founder-led delivery, Australian base, ownership, and a truthful project cue.
- **Visual treatment:** Asymmetric 7/5 or 8/4 grid. Large type on paper; one high-quality project frame partly interrupts the grid. Use cobalt at the action/evidence edge, not as a full decorative wash.
- **Interaction/motion:** Optional two-state project sequence controlled by the visitor, or a single restrained entry reveal. The proposition must remain complete with motion disabled.
- **CTA:** Primary “Start a project”; secondary “View selected work”.
- **Mobile:** Copy first, actions second, evidence third, full-width project media fourth. Target 36–48px display type and avoid a hero taller than roughly 1.5 viewports.

## 3. Proof strip — reasons to believe

- **Goal:** Answer “is this real, relevant, and safe?” before asking for more attention.
- **Likely heading idea:** No marketing heading; use concise evidence labels.
- **Content required:** Three or four substantiated items such as direct founder involvement, end-to-end design/build, client ownership, Australian/remote delivery, a verified result, or named client relationships.
- **Visual treatment:** A ruled band with compact label/value pairs. If client logos are permitted, state the relationship rather than showing a decorative logo cloud.
- **Interaction/motion:** None beyond focus/hover for linked evidence.
- **CTA:** Optional text link to About or Process.
- **Mobile:** Stack as two-by-two or a definition list. Do not create a horizontal auto-scrolling marquee.

## 4. Selected work — proof before catalogue

- **Goal:** Demonstrate visual quality, decision quality, and implementation range.
- **Likely heading idea:** “Selected work” with a short statement about what each project had to change.
- **Content required:** Two to four permissioned projects; project/client, original problem, UI Forge role, platform/discipline, evidence status, outcome, dominant media.
- **Visual treatment:** One large flagship story followed by smaller contrasting stories. Vary composition by evidence—not arbitrary card size. Use project-specific colour inside the stable paper/ink frame.
- **Interaction/motion:** Image-state transitions on deliberate input; project link has clear text and full-card target where appropriate. No hover-only details.
- **CTA:** View all work and per-project “Read the case study”.
- **Mobile:** Full-width media, concise metadata, no tiny side-by-side devices. If only one internal case exists, show it deeply and do not fake a grid.

## 5. Buyer situations and outcomes

- **Goal:** Let visitors recognise the problem they are trying to solve before encountering implementation language.
- **Likely heading idea:** “When the digital experience has to do more than look finished.”
- **Content required:** Three or four situations, such as an unclear or underperforming website, a manual workflow needing a product, a platform decision, or an existing build needing responsible evolution. Pair each with the desired outcome.
- **Visual treatment:** Bordered editorial rows with situation on the left and outcome/decision principle on the right. Use small annotations only when informative.
- **Interaction/motion:** Optional accessible disclosure for supporting detail; keep the core meaning visible.
- **CTA:** Contextual route to Services.
- **Mobile:** Each problem/outcome pair becomes a compact block. Avoid accordion-only access to all useful content.

## 6. Three service paths

- **Goal:** Make the broad offer legible without implying unlimited capacity or a technology shop.
- **Likely heading idea:** “Three ways the studio can help.”
- **Content required:** Websites & commerce; digital products; ongoing care. Each needs who it is for, typical outcomes, indicative deliverables, one related proof item, and boundary/fit language.
- **Visual treatment:** Three numbered panels or rows, with one dark/inverted emphasis moment. Platform names appear as supporting evidence, not headings.
- **Interaction/motion:** Simple hover/focus affordance. Do not use spinning cards or horizontally hidden content.
- **CTA:** Explore Services; conditional deep links only when service pages are substantial.
- **Mobile:** Single ordered sequence with 44px links. Reduce metadata before reducing outcome clarity.

## 7. Founder-led advantage

- **Goal:** Make accountability and direct collaboration tangible.
- **Likely heading idea:** “The person in the room is the person making the work.”
- **Content required:** Founder name, portrait, concise relevant experience, role through the project, communication model, capacity boundaries, and trusted collaborators if genuine.
- **Visual treatment:** Documentary portrait or working image paired with a calm text block and a small founder-involvement timeline. Avoid a generic “meet the team” layout.
- **Interaction/motion:** Optional image-detail reveal; no decorative portrait parallax.
- **CTA:** About the studio or view the Process.
- **Mobile:** Portrait retains a meaningful crop; founder facts stay concise and readable before the longer biography link.

## 8. Process shown through artefacts

- **Goal:** Reduce delivery risk and show how thinking turns into an owned result.
- **Likely heading idea:** “Clear decisions at every stage.”
- **Content required:** Fit/scoping, definition, design, build, QA, launch/handover; example artefact for selected stages; responsibility and decision cues.
- **Visual treatment:** A horizontal timeline on large screens only if it remains readable; preferably a stepped editorial sequence with real artefacts layered beside it.
- **Interaction/motion:** Selecting a stage may update an artefact and caption. It must be keyboard accessible, announce the selected state, and collapse into a linear story with reduced motion.
- **CTA:** See the full process.
- **Mobile:** Vertical stages; show two or three representative artefacts, not six compressed thumbnails. No sticky timeline that consumes the viewport.

## 9. Verified trust and result story

- **Goal:** Add an external voice after the visitor understands the offer and method.
- **Likely heading idea:** Use the strongest short excerpt as a pull quote, not a generic “What clients say”.
- **Content required:** Verified quote, full attribution/role/company/permission, project link, and one result or relationship detail. If the threshold is not met, substitute a documented project outcome—never a fabricated testimonial.
- **Visual treatment:** One substantial quote/result story with generous space, not a carousel of anonymous fragments.
- **Interaction/motion:** None required. If multiple quotes eventually exist, show a visible list or user-controlled selection.
- **CTA:** Read the related case study.
- **Mobile:** Full quote stays readable; attribution does not collapse into tiny grey text.

## 10. Compact insights coda (conditional)

- **Goal:** Demonstrate practical judgment and support research-stage visitors without extending the homepage unnecessarily.
- **Likely heading idea:** “Useful answers before you choose a platform or partner.”
- **Content required:** Two or three current, maintained articles with topic, title, summary, and update date.
- **Visual treatment:** A simple ruled list, not another large card grid.
- **Interaction/motion:** Text-link feedback only.
- **CTA:** Browse Insights.
- **Mobile:** Single list. Remove the module if publishing becomes inactive or the same articles have already been surfaced elsewhere.

## 11. Project-fit close and contact CTA

- **Goal:** Convert confidence into a low-pressure, well-qualified next step.
- **Likely heading idea:** “Bring the problem, even if the platform is not decided.”
- **Content required:** Best-fit summary; what to include; expected response time; availability if maintained; alternative email/contact; ownership/privacy reassurance.
- **Visual treatment:** Ink field with accessible light text and a darker/lighter cobalt action that meets contrast requirements. The close should feel decisive, not like another card.
- **Interaction/motion:** Clear focus/press states only.
- **CTA:** Start a project.
- **Mobile:** CTA is full-width or near-full-width with ample target height. No fixed bottom bar that covers content unless tested and dismissible.

## 12. Footer

- **Goal:** Provide reliable recovery paths, business identity, and legal trust.
- **Content required:** Core navigation, contact, location/service model, selected social links if active, Privacy, Terms, copyright/entity details.
- **Visual treatment:** Structured columns on desktop and grouped disclosure-free lists on mobile.
- **Interaction/motion:** Standard links with obvious focus states.
- **CTA:** Optional compact project CTA, without duplicating the entire previous section.
- **Mobile:** Maintain readable spacing and target sizes; legal links must not be visually hidden.

## Content dependencies

High-fidelity homepage work should not begin until there is a credible plan for:

- two to four project stories and their permissions;
- founder identity and imagery;
- any result, client, logo, and testimonial evidence;
- service-path boundaries and representative deliverables;
- response time/availability wording that can be maintained;
- production-ready Privacy and Terms content.

## Homepage success checks

- A target user can state what UI Forge does, who it is for, and why it differs after a 10–15 second scan.
- A relevant project is reachable in one interaction from the initial page region.
- No unsupported client, outcome, scale, or satisfaction claim appears.
- The sequence works at 390px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px without horizontal page overflow.
- All controls meet target-size, focus, keyboard, contrast, reduced-motion, and zoom expectations.
- The page is meaningfully shorter than the current mobile homepage and contains no repeated argument disguised as a new section.
