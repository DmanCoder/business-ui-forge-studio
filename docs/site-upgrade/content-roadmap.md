## Recommended publishing sequence

1. Publish the redesign checklist because it supports a new service page and addresses a clear,
   high-risk buying decision.
2. Publish the Shopify cost and maintenance-scope articles to give two commercially distinct
   services their first deep supporting resources.
3. Publish the HubSpot redesign guide once terminology and actual delivery scope are owner-checked.
4. Add application-planning content as relevant proof or real enquiry patterns emerge.

## Quality gate for every article

- The topic maps to a real offered service and a clear reader decision.
- The article adds examples, trade-offs or a reusable framework—not a rewritten search summary.
- Claims can be supported and platform details are current on publication day.
- The title, H1 and introduction satisfy the question without keyword repetition.
- Links point to the most relevant service, one useful supporting article and Process or Work only
  when they genuinely help.
- FAQ content is included only if it improves the visible article; schema is not the reason to add it.
- Categories remain noindex until each has enough substantial material and unique introductory
  value to deserve search visibility.
- The article has an owner and review date so platform guidance does not quietly become stale.

## Proof-led content to add when available

The strongest future content is not another generic article. When permission exists, publish a
detailed external case study with the client's situation, constraints, role, decisions, artefacts
and only verified outcomes. Founder biography and portrait material can also strengthen About and
service pages more than anonymous stock imagery or invented social proof.
